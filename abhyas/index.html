Ways to practise

Your screen stays on while the theka plays, so you can prop your phone up and dance.

Tatkar to the theka

Choose Tatkar and start slow. Keep your footwork even and land each Thei on the beat. Raise the laya only when a whole avartan feels steady.

Padhant with tali and khali

Recite the bols aloud, clap on each tali and wave on khali. Turn on Tali claps to check your hands against the cycle.

Land on sam

Choose Tihai. The phrase runs three times, and the last Thei should fall on the bright bead at the top of the ring.

Practise real compositions

On Layakari, gurus and composers publish parans, tihais and kavitts with full notation, and students practise them at any laya.
